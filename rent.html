How it works

A simple way to stay current on rent.

Payments are processed through TurboTenant, the portal HREI uses to collect rent. Use the email invitation from the portal if this is your first time, or sign in if you already have an account.

1

Open your invitation

Check the email address on your lease for an invitation to the tenant portal. If you cannot find it, email us and we will resend it.

2

Sign in and review charges

Your ledger shows rent, any other charges on your account, and due dates before you pay.

3

Pay or set autopay

Pay the balance or turn on autopay so future rent is submitted on schedule. You will receive a receipt after each payment.

Before you pay

  • The portal shows any processing or convenience fee before you confirm payment.
  • A bank transfer (ACH) is typically less expensive than a debit or credit card.
  • Late-fee terms follow your signed lease. If a charge looks wrong, email us before you pay.
  • This page is for current HREI tenants. To ask about upcoming rentals, visit our Rentals page or email leasing.

Need help?

For payment questions, a missing invitation, or a reasonable accommodation request, email offers@hreinvestor.com.

Please include your full name and the property address in your message. Do not send bank account or card numbers by email.
