Information we collect

We collect information you give us, such as your name, phone number, email address, property address, property details, and messages. When you use the members area, we also process the account information needed to provide access.

How we use it

We use this information to respond to property inquiries, evaluate potential transactions, communicate about an inquiry, operate our CRM and members area, protect against fraud and spam, comply with law, and maintain business records.

Email and phone communications

When you submit the Sell Your House form, we may send one confirmation email and contact you about that inquiry by phone or email. This is not consent to recurring marketing. Marketing emails include a working opt-out link; we honor opt-outs for future marketing messages. You can also ask us not to contact you at any time using the contact information below.

Service providers and disclosures

We use service providers that process information on our behalf to operate the site, CRM, business email, and security systems. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or as part of a lawful business transfer. We do not sell personal information or use it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Retention and security

We keep information only as long as reasonably needed for the purposes above, including legal, tax, dispute-resolution, and recordkeeping needs. We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards, but no system is completely secure.

Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information, subject to applicable law and recordkeeping requirements. You may opt out of marketing emails using the link in the message or by contacting us. We will verify requests before acting on them.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy when our practices or legal obligations change. We will post the updated version here with a new effective date.

Contact
