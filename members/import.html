Bulk Lead Import

Upload a CSV list, review how it will be added, and import only after checking duplicates. Imported phone and email details are never treated as SMS consent.

1. Choose your CSV file

Use the template for the smoothest import. Common column names such as Name, Address, Phone, and Email are also recognized.

Template columns

Only seller name plus one contact or property detail is required.

  • seller_name — owner or seller
  • property_address, city, state, zip
  • phone, email
  • asking_price, notes — optional

Tip: Keep your original spreadsheet. This tool adds leads; it does not overwrite existing records.
